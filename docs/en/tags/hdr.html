Open Test Patterns

A free (Apache-2.0) generator of exact test patterns for grading and HDR QC: SMPTE RP 219 bars, ColorChecker, PQ steps, zone plates, grids, plus audio test tones, exported as EXR, DPX, TIFF or PNG in the resolution and color space you need.

Found via: Digital Production — Open Test Patterns joins ASWF DPEL
