MusE 4.3

The free MIDI/audio sequencer (GPL-2.0) for Linux releases 4.3 on Oct 19, three years after 4.2.1: variable note-name lists (microtonal), adjustable middle C, a reworked advanced routing dialog, a fully undoable effect rack and many VST/LV2 fixes. Last Qt5 release.

SoundCraft 0.8.0

A free DAW (Apache-2.0) in Rust following the Pro Tools workflow, Edit and Mix windows, with CLAP/VST3/AU plugins, automation, MIDI, up to 9.1.6 surround and a picture track to sync sound to video. Linux, macOS, Windows, FreeBSD and browser; 0.8.0 out Oct 18.
