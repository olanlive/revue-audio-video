oscmix 2.0.11

Free mixer for RME Fireface interfaces (UCX II, UFX III, 802…) in class-compliant mode, where TotalMix doesn’t exist on Linux. 2.0.11 (pre-release, Oct 15) adds 8 fader groups, two aux FX buses and a web UI that keeps its state on reload.

Found via: LinuxMusicians — oscmix, RME TotalMix alternative for Linux CC-mode

October 17, 2026

ChipTune (beta)

Morphoice synth recreating the Commodore 64 SID chip, bit-accurately modelled at the original clock (6581 and 8580): solo, chiptune arpeggio and 16-voice poly modes, Juno-style chorus. Free pay-what-you-want beta (Oct 16); the final version may be paid.

Found via: Rekkerd — FREE: ChipTune C64 SID chip emulation (public beta)

Fish Audio Drama 3 (fish-speech 3.0.0 beta)

Open-weights “directed” speech synthesis model (Oct 13): voice cloning, acting cues ([laughs], <whisper>), retaking a line from a simple note, scenes with up to 16 voices and editing one word in existing audio. ~80 languages; 48 GB GPU required.
